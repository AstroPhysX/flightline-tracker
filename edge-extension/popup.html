Flightline Sync

Open UPS Time Detail from the first scheduled flight/date and click Sync current page. The extension first tries a one-click screen capture. If that cannot be read reliably, it asks you to use Ctrl+A / Ctrl+C as a fallback.

Safety / privacy

The extension never logs in, handles MFA, clicks UPS controls, polls UPS in the background, reads UPS cookies, or sends requests to UPS. A screen capture happens only after you press Sync. It is sent only to your configured self-hosted Flightline Tracker for in-memory OCR and is not stored. If OCR is uncertain, no schedule change is made and the extension falls back to copied text.
